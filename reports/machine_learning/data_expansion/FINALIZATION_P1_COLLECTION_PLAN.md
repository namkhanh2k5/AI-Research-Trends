# Finalization Phase 1 — bounded OpenAlex collection plan

The versioned local plan is `data/metadata/finalization_phase1_collection_plan.json` (Git-ignored); its 80 exact topic/year strata, source populations, seeds, requested counts, filters and per-stratum capacities are summarized in [quota detail](../../finalization_p1/collection_quotas_by_topic_year.csv). The planner checked complete OpenAlex `group_by=publication_year` responses for 20 topic/broad sources and stored every response with query and SHA-256 under `data/analysis/finalization_p1_source_counts_20261101_v1/`. These are *capacity checks*, not a new AI population estimate.

The approved query frame is 2020–2023 only, exact annual publication-date boundaries, types `article|review|preprint`, `primary_topic.subfield.id:1702|1707`, and `is_retracted:false`. Selected strata additionally require `primary_topic.id:<verified topic ID>`. This is the defined AI/CV primary-subfield corpus, not all worldwide AI research. Topic IDs and descriptions come from the verified Phase 6B catalogue and OpenAlex [topics documentation](https://help.openalex.org/data/topics/). The source-side `sample`, `seed`, `page` and `per_page` parameters follow the [OpenAlex pagination guidance](https://help.openalex.org/api/paging/); a seeded server sample is **not** asserted to have known inclusion probabilities.

An independent read-only rebuild of the saved plan against all 21 hashed raw capacity responses and the gap CSV returned **PASS: 80 verified strata, 2,515 planned records**. Reproduce with `openalex-plan-ml-expansion --verify-plan data/metadata/finalization_phase1_collection_plan.json --gap-csv data/analysis/finalization_p1_gap_20261101_v1/data_gap.csv --capacity-dir data/analysis/finalization_p1_source_counts_20261101_v1`. This rejects changed source checksums, malformed year groups, and altered allocation or quota totals.

The selected-topic quota is proportional to estimated raw need and distributed across years by available source counts using largest remainder with capacity enforcement. Outside sources were chosen before any 2024/2025 label inspection. The broad sample may contain selected topics; post-collection training roles are assigned by the **actual primary topic**, not by the query source. The [per-year quotas](../../finalization_p1/collection_quotas_by_year.csv) are 558, 608, 620, and 729 for 2020–2023, 20 pages per year. The largest stratum requests 76 works, below the [OpenAlex per-page limit of 100](https://help.openalex.org/api/paging/). Planned total: **80 list requests**, maximum **180 requests/180 observed credits**, maximum **3,000 raw records**. Capacity discovery consumed 21 observed credits separately; 120 singleton drift checks reported zero credits in response headers. The current pricing and credit policies should always be checked against [OpenAlex access information](https://help.openalex.org/access/pricing/) before another run.

The collector writes immutable raw page bytes atomically, hashes each page, and checkpoints a manifest. It rejects invalid corpus members, short sample pages, duplicate Work IDs within a page, changed plan/config hashes, and completed-run resume. On resume, **all checkpointed pages** are re-fetched and their ordered Work IDs compared before new pages are appended; mismatch marks the run incomplete and retains raw/probe evidence. This mitigates, but cannot prove absence of OpenAlex source updates or a consistent snapshot between pages. Orphan/partial page evidence stops the run for manual inspection rather than being silently replaced. Request failures preserve an incomplete manifest. The planned request cap includes normal pages and any resume probes.

A separate **36-work smoke plan** across all four years and three source types was executed first. Its 12 pages, raw/interim SHA-256, exact corpus membership and 36 distinct IDs passed read-only verification. The production run then executed under its own directories; actual outcomes are in the [expansion report](FINALIZATION_P1_EXPANSION_REPORT.md). No 2024 or 2025 works were acquired in this expansion.
