Inspected the historical pre-remediation `deliverables/presentation/FINAL_PRESENTATION.pptx`, `.pdf`, `DEMO_SCRIPT.md` and slide generator. `unzip -t` passed PPTX; `pdfinfo` reported **12 slides**, 16:9-sized 959.981×540 pt PDF, 586,889 bytes. Slide text was extracted with `pdftotext -layout`; rendered-page audit captures were later removed as disposable evidence. These findings describe the older deck, not the later 15-slide deliverable; universal clipping absence was **NOT VERIFIED**.

Content: slides 1–2 establish purpose and the three dataset scopes. Slides 3–6 summarize RQ1–RQ4 with actual published figures and correct headline numbers. Slides 7–8 cover RQ5/RQ6 but have no figures. Slide 9 compares models and is consistent with the 681-case, development-only SVM result; slide 10 states 21.66% outside false acceptance; 11–12 give limits/conclusion. The RQ3 slide correctly labels an unadjusted β−.330, adjusted Gold β+.463 CI [.396,.531], Green β+.178 CI [.087,.269] and noncausality. It does not claim 58.9% more raw citations. Paper collection and preprocessing are not visually explained: no API/Parquet flow or table-grain diagram. No screenshot of the actual website appears in the deck. Figures cite only a generic footer (“source in report”) rather than per-figure short attribution. Some slides use 3–4 multi-line bullets; viewed slides remain readable but a 5–7 minute talk may be dense.

`DEMO_SCRIPT.md` specifies seven timed sections (0:00–6:30) covering Home, RQ1–RQ4, Topic Explorer, Paper Explorer, Classify and Methodology. Home, Research Landscape, topic browsing and paper search were actually opened in Chrome; RQ3/RQ4 tabs clicked. The Classify script action “confirm one topic” **FAILED/NOT COMPLETED** because a real prediction raises `KeyError: 'topic_name'` before the confirmation control. The script's final “open academic report” is also blocked by the Methodology link routing to Streamlit HTML rather than the document. Therefore the claimed 5–7-minute full live demo was **not successfully rehearsed**; the estimated 6.5-minute timing remains only the author's schedule, not measured performance. A safe presentation can use slide-only fallback, but that is not equivalent to successful interactive demonstration.

Recommended later: repair and retest confirmation/link flow, capture real UI screenshots, add one succinct preprocessing/architecture visual, attribute figures briefly on-slide, then time an uninterrupted rehearsal with one synthetic non-test draft. This audit made no deck changes.
