# Phase 6B decision criteria (fixed before expanded-model results)

These thresholds are project-specific decision aids, not scientific laws. Phase 6A showed a five-class macro-F1 of 0.953 for Linear SVM but only 20.2% 2024 catalogue coverage; the new test must balance **scope, coverage, learnability and rejection**. Human validity remains a separate gate.

1. **Catalogue:** 8–15 provisionally defensible AI/CV topics, each with at least 70 train and 20 validation works, and ≥30% of all text-eligible 2024 works covered. The 30% target is a pragmatic floor for a *bounded topic-placement tool*, not all-AI/CV coverage. No 2025 selection evidence.
2. **Closed set:** best supervised macro-F1 ≥0.75 on the expanded 2024 task and ≥0.10 above the strongest executable baseline; no selected class with zero recall. Training-fold variation and classwise support must be disclosed. Five-class and expanded scores are not the same estimand.
3. **Open set:** on a held-out 2024 evaluation subset after calibration-only threshold selection, aim for ≥80% acceptance of in-catalogue works and ≤20% false acceptance of outside works. Also disclose AUROC, accepted precision/accuracy, end-to-end correct placement over **all** eligible evaluated works, and selective risk/coverage. Failure means no unrestricted topic finder, regardless of closed-set F1.
4. **SVM sanity:** no material cross-period duplicate leakage or privileged feature, plus a complete classwise error audit and an explanation of dominant lexical features. If near-duplicate sensitivity materially changes results, use the corrected estimate for decision-making.
5. **Semantic claims:** human catalogue review and actual independent annotations are required for a fully validated scientific classifier. Without them, at most a **technical GO — human validation pending** for a clearly limited educational workflow; never claim semantic accuracy.
6. **Product/reproducibility:** offline Topic Brief works on real precomputed statistics, handles invalid/out-of-catalogue input, and labels sample versus population sources. All source/output hashes and relevant tests pass. A frozen final protocol is required before one-time 2025 technical evaluation.

**GO:** all quantitative gates and product checks pass; state human limitation explicitly. **REVISE:** a narrow five-topic teaching demonstration or label-reliability tool is supportable, but expansion or open-set behavior fails; choose one bounded path, not endless tuning. **NO-GO:** even narrowed labels or product claims lack defensible evidence, or further work would endanger project completion. Do not change these thresholds after viewing expanded-model results; deviations must be labelled post hoc.
